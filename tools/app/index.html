Your layout

    does…

    Picking the button’s own job puts it back to default. To swap two buttons, change both.

    Remap without a computer (Quick Remap)
    1. Hold Power + Start for 1.5 s. The LED pulses purple and the controller ticks once; the game sees the controller at rest.
    2. Press the button you want to change (one tick), then the button whose job it should do (two ticks, green flash). Saved at once.
    3. Hold Start for 3 s to reset every button (orange flash).
    4. Tap Power to finish (or leave it alone for 20 s).

      Going back to official firmware

      1. Unplug the controller. Hold ZL + Start and plug it in: a drive appears.
      2. Drag the official gcu_r4k.uf2 onto it. The controller restarts on its own.
